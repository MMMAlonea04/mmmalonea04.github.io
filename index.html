About

Two disciplines, one workflow

I work across the full stack: JavaScript and CSS on the front end, Python services and notebooks on the back end, and the glue in between. Most of what I ship starts as a rough idea, becomes a working prototype, then gets hardened into something people can rely on.

On the data side I spend my time in pandas, exploratory notebooks and Streamlit dashboards — turning messy datasets into numbers that answer a question. I'm equally comfortable debugging a layout at 360px and arguing about a train/test split.
